Proposal · Engineering

Establish AI-native engineering culture at MiracleSoft

Summary
MiracleSoft's AI adoption has outrun our governance, delivery, and coaching infrastructure. This memo proposes a six-pillar framework to codify AI-native engineering at MiracleSoft, and a Forward Deployed AI-Native Coach as the role that makes it operational. The framework is the product. The role exists to make it real.
01 · The opportunity

AI tooling is a commodity. Culture is the moat.

Every competitor licenses the same models, the same agents, the same IDEs. What sets a high-performing engineering org apart in 2026 is practice — how work is planned, executed, reviewed, and shared.

Today MiracleSoft has capable individual adopters and no shared framework. Token spend is invisible. Prompt patterns vanish when engineers rotate off accounts. Compliance exposure grows quietly. The AI-forward talent we want to hire cannot tell from the outside whether we take this seriously.

The cost of waiting another quarter is drift, duplication, and talent leakage.

02 · The framework

Six pillars of AI-native engineering at MiracleSoft

Each pillar is concrete, measurable, and enforced by tooling. Together they define how a MiracleSoft squad plans, executes, reviews, and shares AI-driven work across the Global Delivery Model.

01
Plan before inference
Substantive AI-driven work begins with a Plan PR — scope, category, estimated cost, approach. Assigned category (scaffold, refactor, debug, explore, test, document, review) drives token budget tier and reviewer. Reviewed before any inference runs. Small tasks pass through; we pick our spots.
02
Discovery-first kickoff
Every new project opens with a 3-day discovery session. Team-level token pool, no individual caps. Full creative freedom across models, agents, and tools. Deliverables: working demos, approach recommendation, risk notes. Prevents premature convergence and surfaces the best ideas before execution pressure builds.
03
Outcome-weighted scorecard
Weekly per-engineer metrics: tokens consumed, sessions run, Plan PR quality, and token-to-value ratio. Ratio matters more than volume — so the scorecard rewards efficiency, not just usage. Feeds coaching, staffing, and promotion decisions.
04
Context and shared tooling
Every repository ships with a project instruction file (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, or equivalent). Engineers maintain personal context files. A published context engineering standard treats prompts and agent definitions with the rigor we apply to code. An org-maintained sub-agent and command library lets squads pull, customize, and contribute back. All versioned in git.
05
Guardrails and evaluation gates
Pre-commit hooks and CI checks enforce the framework: Plan PR exists, category assigned, budget respected, context updated, AI-generated code passes evaluation before merge. Critical for client delivery. Compliance becomes the path of least resistance.
06
Delivery ledger
Internal record of AI-driven shipped work across accounts: which squad shipped what, which patterns worked, measurable velocity and quality gains. Turns individual wins into org-wide knowledge and makes AI impact visible to leadership.
03 · Why a dedicated role

Culture does not run itself

Every pillar above is cross-cutting. No squad owns Plan PR standards, the task taxonomy, or the shared agent library. Side-of-desk maintenance gets deprioritized the moment client delivery pressure rises. The framework becomes a document no one opens.

A Forward Deployed AI-Native Coach operates the framework: curating the taxonomy, reviewing Plan PRs, leading discovery sessions, stewarding the shared library, and embedding with squads to coach adoption. Hands-on technical leadership — success measured by framework adoption and squad productivity. Full role description is in tab 03.

04 · Business impact

What the framework delivers for a professional services business

MiracleSoft's business is billable engineering delivery at scale — 2,500+ Miraclites executing across 44 Fortune 100 accounts through Agile-as-a-Service squads, Rapid60 engagements, and our Centers of Excellence. Every lever in the framework maps to the economics of that business.

Billable delivery margin
Scaffolding, shared sub-agents, and Plan PR discipline raise output per billable hour. On Agile-as-a-Service engagements, even modest productivity gains translate to margin expansion — or sharper pricing on contested bids. In a services business, utilization is the product.
Win rate on AI RFPs
Clients in BFSI, Healthcare, Insurance, and Manufacturing now ask how we govern AI before they sign. A published, version-controlled framework is a bid asset most competitors cannot match. Revenue lever, not internal hygiene.
Token cost visibility
Inference is a material cost on every AI engagement. The scorecard gives MiracleSoft visibility that most services firms won't have for another 12 months — protecting gross margin on fixed-fee work as AI spend grows.
miraAI and Rapid60
The framework is a living reference implementation for miraAI inside MiracleSoft, and provides the execution discipline that makes Rapid60 engagements repeatable rather than heroic. Our own delivery becomes the proof point for our own IP.
Global delivery consistency
Squads across the US, India, and our other geographies adopt AI tooling at different rates. A shared framework, scaffolding CLI, and instruction file convention deliver consistent quality and real cross-squad mobility.
CoE multiplier
The shared library plugs into every Center of Excellence — Cloud, Data, Integration, Automation. One investment, leverage across practices.
Talent pipeline
AI-native engineers signal-check employers before applying. A credible published framework says MiracleSoft is serious. That signal compounds in recruitment and retention, where replacing a senior Miraclite runs into six figures.
Job description

Forward Deployed AI-Native Coach

Team
Engineering, Practice Management, Pre-sales
Location
Novi, Michigan (hybrid)
Reports to
Engineering leadership
Overview

Turn AI governance from aspiration into practice

You will define and operate a first-of-its-kind function at MiracleSoft — the function that makes AI-native culture real across squads, accounts, and CoEs. You will own the framework, ship the tooling that enforces it, coach the engineers who live inside it, and stay hands-on with code, context, and agents.

You are forward-deployed to wherever the friction is. Your work directly shapes how MiracleSoft ships software in the AI era.

What you will drive

Core responsibilities

Framework ownership
Own the six-pillar governance framework end-to-end. Publish, maintain, and evolve it based on squad feedback, account learnings, and industry shifts.
Plan PR review and gatekeeping
Review substantive Plan PRs before inference runs. Ensure scope, category, budget, and approach are clear. Set the bar others follow.
Discovery session leadership
Design and facilitate 3-day discovery sessions at project kickoff. Coach squads through creative exploration; consolidate outputs into actionable approach recommendations.
Shared library stewardship
Curate the org-wide sub-agent and slash command library. Review contributions, enforce quality, keep the catalog current with model and tool changes.
Context engineering standards
Author and maintain the MiracleSoft context engineering standard. Review project and personal instruction files. Elevate context from afterthought to engineering artifact.
AI-readiness coaching
Use the scorecard to identify coaching opportunities. Run 1:1s and small-group workshops. Help engineers move from AI-curious to AI-fluent.
Guardrail and evaluation tooling
Partner with platform engineering to build the enforcement layer: pre-commit hooks, CI checks, Plan PR templates, scaffolding CLI, evaluation harnesses for client-facing code.
Delivery ledger curation
Collect, verify, and publish AI-driven wins across accounts. Write the internal comms that turn individual shipping into org-wide knowledge.
Cross-team bridge
Translate field-level friction into roadmap input for miraAI and Rapid60. Partner with engineering, product, security, and account leadership.
Hands-on technical contribution
Write code. Review PRs. Debug production issues. Credibility in this role is earned in the work.

First 180 days

First 12 months
Measurable token-to-value improvement across squads. Inbound AI-forward applicants cite the framework. Function ready to expand.
