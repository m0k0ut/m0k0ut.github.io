Marchex · Engage for Sales & Service
AI conversation intelligence
📞
What it does
AI analyzes inbound calls to classify intent, detect missed opportunities, and score sentiment across sales and service interactions at scale.

FordDirect angle
Available to ~3,000 Ford & Lincoln dealers through FordDirect. Feeds call outcomes and sentiment into performance reporting and coaching; enables re‑engagement on high‑value missed calls.
  • Engage for Sales & Service on top of prior Spotlight call analytics.
  • Dealer‑level insights into revenue leakage and CX quality.

Lifecycle concierge
💬

AI concierge that engages shoppers and owners across digital channels, with bi‑directional data flow to FordDirect's Customer Journey Platform (CJP).

FordDirect angle
Impel is integrated directly into CJP so that first‑party data informs conversations, and AI conversations feed back rich self‑reported data.
  • Hyper‑personalized outreach across sales and service touchpoints.
  • Material lifts in show appointments and incremental repair orders in early pilots.

Ford AI Assistant
Vehicle‑aware AI thread
🧠
Built on Google Cloud with access to LLMs, the Ford AI Assistant is designed as an "intelligent thread" across app and in‑vehicle experiences. It is aware of vehicle context (equipment, health, capabilities) as well as general knowledge.
  • Launches in Ford & Lincoln apps first (H1 2026), then into vehicles on UEV platform.
  • Can answer questions about your specific vehicle (tire pressure, towing, range, etc.).
  • Supports text, voice, and image input (e.g., cargo fit questions).
8M+ customers app reach Context‑aware assistant
PM takeaway: hook assistant signals into service outreach and loyalty flows.
Systems detail: assistant sits on GCP with LLM access + vehicle context APIs; plan API contracts early.
UEV Platform & Compute Module
Software‑defined hardware
🧩
Ford's new Universal EV (UEV) platform underpins a ~$30K EV truck and future models. A compact, in‑house‑designed compute module consolidates infotainment, ADAS, audio, and connectivity into a single "vehicle brain."
  • ~20% fewer parts, 15% faster assembly, lower cost base.
  • Smaller, cheaper compute with much tighter control over critical semiconductors.
  • Deliberate vertical integration to own the AI + autonomy stack over time.
Centralized compute Vertical integration
PM takeaway: cheaper, centralized compute = faster OTA AI features and lower BOM for value models.
Systems detail: consolidated ECU + ADAS stack; design for single OTA pipeline and telemetry schemas.
Next‑Gen BlueCruise
From L2 to L3 (eyes‑off)
🛣️
BlueCruise is Ford's hands‑free driving system, already deployed on over a million vehicles with hundreds of millions of hands‑free miles. Ford is now driving toward Level 3 "eyes‑off" capabilities on the UEV platform.
  • Next‑gen system targets ~30% lower cost via new compute and architecture.
  • L3 eyes‑off driving targeted around 2028 for everyday customers, not just flagship models.
  • Subscription monetization model already in place; opens path for more AI subscriptions.
ADAS → autonomy Subscription revenue
PM takeaway: monetize via subscriptions + coaching; use driving data to personalize offers.
Systems detail: L3 roadmap hinges on new compute & sensor fusion; align data contracts for behavior signals.

Internal AI Platforms: FordLLM & Mach1ML
The internal "rails" that make Ford's AI products shippable at enterprise scale.
Internal platform leverage
FordLLM
Enterprise LLM portal
🗂️
FordLLM is the internal generative AI portal giving employees access to multiple LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Llama) via a secure UI and APIs. Adoption has reached tens of thousands of weekly active users across functions.
  • Used for market research, documentation, code assistance, and content creation.
  • Supports both individual productivity and programmatic integration into applications.
Secure LLM access APIs for apps
PM takeaway: leverage existing LLM UI/APIs instead of net-new stacks; focus on use-case fit.
Systems detail: multi-model gateway with policy guardrails; design intents + retrieval layers on top.
Mach1ML & MDK
MLOps & model factory
⚙️
Mach1ML is Ford's multi‑million‑dollar ML platform powering predictive and generative use cases. Its MDK (MLOps Development Kit) uses Apache Airflow and custom operators to orchestrate training, evaluation, and deployment across cloud and HPC.
  • Standardized pipelines, experiment tracking, and deployment patterns.
  • Supports both batch and real‑time models for customer, engineering, and operations use cases.
Standardized ML pipelines HPC + cloud hybrid
PM takeaway: ask platform for reusable features (churn, propensity) before bespoke models.
Systems detail: Airflow + custom operators for train/eval/deploy; supports batch + streaming endpoints.

How to Tell the Story as a Sales & Services AI PM
Connect dealer AI, vehicle AI, and internal platforms into one coherent narrative.
Interview cheat sheet
Core narrative
Ford is already rich with AI components: conversation intelligence (Marchex), conversational engagement (Impel, Podium), dealer co‑pilots (Insight Engage), vehicle‑side AI (Ford AI Assistant, BlueCruise), and internal platforms (FordLLM, Mach1ML). The opportunity of the Sales & Services AI PM role is to make these pieces feel like one continuous, predictive, personalized journey rather than separate tools.
Example product vision you can articulate
"I'd build a Customer Intelligence Engine on top of CJP that continuously fuses signals from connected vehicles, dealer interactions, and digital behavior to generate real‑time customer profiles. Those profiles would power: (1) a proactive service engine that reaches out before issues surface, (2) a dealer co‑pilot that surfaces next‑best actions during every interaction, and (3) loyalty personalization that optimizes lifetime value. It would be built on Mach1ML, expose experiences via Ford AI Assistant and dealer tools, and be governed by Ford's Trust Algorithm."
Business metrics
Lead‑to‑sale conversion, service revenue per VIN, churn / retention, NPS / CSAT, dealer adoption.
Technical levers
CJP event model, Marchex intents, Impel/Podium transcripts, FordLLM orchestration, Mach1ML models.
